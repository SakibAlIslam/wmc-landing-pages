Capturing the Back-to-School opportunity at Walmart

The Back-to-School season always arrives faster than expected, and this year will be no different. As parents and students prepare for the upcoming academic year, it’s essential to be ready to connect with shoppers during this key retail moment.

Class is back in session!

Canada’s Back-to-School season represents a $4 billion retail opportunity. With spend per child averaging anywhere between $600–$750 and most anticipating to shop 2–3 times to complete their shopping lists, there’s no shortage of opportunities to engage with shoppers.¹

Walmart plays a key role for Back-to-School shoppers

Back-to-School shoppers are turning to mass retailers to check off items on their lists, with price remaining a top consideration. Over half of shoppers say that finding good prices and deals is a challenge, and more than one-third say staying on budget is difficult.¹

The summer months are important shopping periods across categories

Walmart shoppers complete their purchases across categories in waves. Even within the same category, the channel influences when we see shoppers making their purchases in preparation for Back-to-School season.

Online

Online, the month of July is key for Electronics, School Supplies, and Home items. August is where we see the most lift within the Apparel category year over year.¹

In-store

July is key for Apparel and Electronics shoppers in store, with Home and School Supplies seeing more activity in August.¹

Early and consistent placements can help you stay top of mind all season long to capture attention and boost sales!
